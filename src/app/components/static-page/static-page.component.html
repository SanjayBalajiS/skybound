Find quick answers to our most common questions below.

How early should I arrive at the airport?

For domestic flights, we recommend arriving 2 hours prior to departure. For international flights, please arrive at least 3 hours before.

What is the carry-on baggage allowance?

All passengers are allowed one cabin bag (up to 7kg) and one small personal item (e.g., laptop bag or purse) free of charge.

Can I change my flight online?

Yes! Navigate to "My Bookings" in the mega-menu, view your booking, and select "Change Flight". Note that change fees may apply depending on your fare class.
